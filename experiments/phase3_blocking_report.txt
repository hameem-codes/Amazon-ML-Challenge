Interpretation:
Exact Name blocking generates very few candidates (high precision, low candidate volume) but suffers from low recall because any typo, formatting difference, or missing word breaks the match.
Adding Country to Exact Name blocking slightly reduces candidate volume further but doesn't improve recall.
Token blocking significantly increases recall by matching entities sharing at least one word, but the candidate volume explodes (due to common tokens like 'Inc', 'LLC', 'Private').
Character 3-gram blocking achieves the highest recall, finding matches even with typos, but produces an astronomical number of candidates, making it computationally expensive without filtering.
