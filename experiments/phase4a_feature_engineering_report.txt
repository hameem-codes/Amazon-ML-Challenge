8. METHODOLOGY & OBSERVATIONS:
- Missingness Handling: Missing names or addresses explicitly activate directional missingness indicators (e.g. s1_name_missing, candidate_address_missing) while setting similarity features to 0.0, avoiding spurious high-similarity signals on nulls.
- Country Handling: Open-set country comparison checks equality without hardcoding specific country lists, making it robust for France, US, India, or other countries.
- Blocking Evidence: Incorporates how candidates were retrieved into the feature matrix without rerunning expensive blocking inside the feature extractor.
- Ground Truth Separation: Ground truth labels are generated separately and never fed as feature inputs.
- Imbalance: In the 1,000 S1 candidate pool, positive rate is ~0.87%, giving an imbalance ratio of ~114:1 negatives to positives. This will be addressed during XGBoost loss/weight tuning.
